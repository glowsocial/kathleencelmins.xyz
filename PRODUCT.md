# Kathleen Celmins

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Kathleen writes here. Visitors read her published writing and can subscribe by email.

## Product Purpose

Kathleen wants this site to feel like a place where she wants to write. She confirmed that the site's feel is the main barrier. This redesign changes the reading experience and visual identity.

## Operating Context

The existing Next.js site reads published Markdown from `content/writing/`. Private material stays in the ignored `writing-workbench/` folders. Kathleen approves each piece before publication.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve published writing, archive content, URLs, RSS, email subscription, light and dark themes, and the existing writing tools. Do not publish private drafts. The redesign does not need a new editor, account system, or writing schedule.

## Brand Commitments

The site belongs to Kathleen Celmins. Her writing includes business, books, life, and personal thought. Use plain words. Do not invent biographical claims or rewrite her published prose.

## Evidence on Hand

Published essays are in `content/writing/`. Recovered public writing is in `content/archive/`. Existing portraits are in `public/images/`. Existing About, Goals, and Work with me pages contain factual copy.

## Product Principles

- Let the writing lead.
- Make room for short pieces as well as long essays.
- Keep subscription and business content secondary to reading.
- Preserve Kathleen's control over publication.

## Delivery

Kathleen said, "it's such a throwaway just do it live." Build directly and publish the checked redesign. Do not add another design decision or preview approval step.
