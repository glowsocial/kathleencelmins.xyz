# Personal writing site

Scope: home, shared frame, and reading typography. Mode: Read.

Kathleen identified feel as the barrier and asked for direct live delivery. Keep every published piece, URL, private draft boundary, subscription endpoint, and RSS feed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal commonplace book. Reading starts beside a small introduction. The page gives a short piece the same dignity as an essay and does not open with a subscription pitch.

OWN-WORLD: A sage-green endpaper field, forest ink, a quiet off-white reading ground, Source Serif 4 titles, and small plain sans-serif navigation. Open space separates entries. No boxed post cards. Light mode serves daytime writing; the existing dark-mode preference becomes a soft green-black reading room.

STORY: Meet Kathleen, read a real piece, follow another thought, and optionally subscribe at the end. Business links remain available in the footer and About page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A restrained name and three navigation links sit above two unequal columns. The left column has the existing "Everything, in writing" line and a brief personal introduction. The right column begins with the newest real post. A post title underline changes on hover and keyboard focus. Mobile stacks the short introduction above the writing without hiding the navigation.

FORM: Personal commonplace book, grounded candidate 4, seed 2da8d00a. User direction supersedes the concept-selection ceremony. Build directly; no comp approval.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
