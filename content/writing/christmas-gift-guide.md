I'm already thinking about Christmas. Truth be told, I have been thinking about it since October 1. I'm doing homemade gifts again this year, after a brief hiatus.

This year is all about caramel sauce. But I'm mixing it up a bit and adding new flavors.

I put a bunch of links in the Notes app, which actually wasn't helpful AT ALL as a place to reference things.

If for some reason someone other than my husband stumbles upon this post, I have two girls in elementary school, ages 7 and 10.
